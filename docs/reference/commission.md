# Commission

## What Is It?

A **commission** is an explicit fee charged by a broker to execute a trade on your behalf. It's the most visible cost of trading.

---

## Common Commission Structures

| Broker Type | Structure | Example |
|------------|-----------|---------|
| **Per-share** | Fixed rate per share | $0.005/share (IBKR Pro) |
| **Per-trade** | Flat fee per order | $6.95/trade (legacy brokers) |
| **Zero-commission** | $0 explicit fee | Robinhood, Webull, Schwab |
| **Tiered** | Rate decreases with volume | $0.0035/share for first 300K shares/month |

---

## Zero-Commission ≠ Free Trading

"Commission-free" brokers earn revenue through:
- **Payment for Order Flow (PFOF)** — see pfof.md
- **Interest on uninvested cash**
- **Securities lending**
- **Premium subscription fees**

The hidden costs may exceed what you'd pay in explicit commissions.

---

## Impact on P&L

Zorivest tracks commissions per trade and computes:

- **Commission as % of P&L**: How much of your profit/loss was consumed by fees.
- **Fee Summary**: Aggregate commission costs across all trades, filterable by period.

High commission-to-profit ratios on small trades indicate you may be overtrading or trading too small.

## How Zorivest Uses It

The **Fees** tab in Trade Detail shows the commission paid on each trade and its percentage impact on P&L. The **Costs & Mistakes** tab in Analytics aggregates fees across your entire portfolio.
