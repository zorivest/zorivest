# Quarterly Estimated Tax Payments

## What Are They?

**Estimated tax payments** are quarterly payments to the IRS for income that doesn't have tax withheld (e.g., capital gains from trading, self-employment income). The US tax system is pay-as-you-go — you're expected to pay taxes throughout the year, not just at filing time.

---

## Who Must Pay?

You generally must make estimated tax payments if:
- You expect to owe **$1,000 or more** in tax after subtracting withholding and credits.
- Your withholding and credits will be less than the **lesser of**:
  - 90% of current year tax, or
  - 100% of prior year tax (110% if AGI > $150,000)

Active traders with significant realized gains almost always need to make estimated payments.

---

## Due Dates

| Quarter | Income Period | Due Date |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| Q1 | Jan 1 – Mar 31 | **April 15** |
| Q2 | Apr 1 – May 31 | **June 15** |
| Q3 | Jun 1 – Aug 31 | **September 15** |
| Q4 | Sep 1 – Dec 31 | **January 15** (next year) |

Note: Q2 and Q3 cover unequal periods (2 and 3 months respectively).

---

## Safe Harbor Methods

Two methods to avoid underpayment penalties:

| Method | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Prior Year Safe Harbor** | Pay 100% of last year's tax ÷ 4 per quarter (110% if AGI > $150K) |
| **Annualized Income Method** | Pay 90% of current year's estimated tax, annualized per quarter |

The prior year method is simpler — you know the amount in advance regardless of current-year gains.

---

## Underpayment Penalties

If you don't pay enough by each quarterly deadline:
- **Penalty rate**: Federal short-term rate + 3 percentage points (updated quarterly).
- **Penalty calculation**: Applied per quarter on the underpayment amount.
- Penalties are computed per quarter — paying extra in Q4 doesn't fully offset Q1 underpayment.

## How Zorivest Uses It

The **Quarterly** tab tracks:
- **Required payment**: Computed from your Tax Profile using either annualized or prior-year method.
- **Actual payment**: Recorded manually when you make a payment.
- **Remaining due**: Required − Paid.
- **Underpayment risk**: Flags quarters where you may owe a penalty.
