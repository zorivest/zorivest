# Zorivest user guide
<!-- pack: improving-investor-v1@1.0.0 -->

This guide teaches Zorivest on its built-in sample, the Improving Investor. The sample is a complete, fictional trading journal: accounts, executions, plans, reviews, watchlists, tax cases, AI reviews and stored reports, all frozen at one point in time. Every chapter points at real rows in that sample, so you can open the app beside the guide and see exactly what the text describes.

Zorivest plans and evaluates trades. It never places, changes or cancels orders, and the sample contains no broker connection.

Synthetic sample data. Not affiliated with any named institution.

## Chapters

Start with the walkthrough, then read the topic chapters in any order.

1. [The sample walkthrough](sample-walkthrough.md) — open the sample and see the whole journal in ten minutes.
2. [Accounts and plans](01-accounts-and-plans.md) — the five accounts, their balance history and the trade plans.
3. [Trades](02-trades.md) — the executions, a stock split and the same ticker in two accounts.
4. [Spotlights and reviews](03-spotlights-and-reviews.md) — ten teaching trades and a year of the trader's own reviews.
5. [AI reviews](04-ai-reviews.md) — eight frozen AI trade reviews you can read without an AI provider.
6. [Watchlists as a scratch pad](05-watchlists-scratch-pad.md) — tickers with one plain note each.
7. [Tax and wash sales](06-tax-and-wash-sales.md) — five loss sales and how the wash-sale rule treats each one.
8. [Analytics and evidence](07-analytics-and-evidence.md) — strategy expectancy, what needs market data, and cited news.
9. [Stored reports](08-stored-reports.md) — twenty frozen reports: daily briefs, weekly reviews, monthly digests and a quarterly tax check.
10. [Activating your sample reports](09-activating-sample-reports.md) — turn a sample report policy into one of your own.

## How to read a chapter

- **Names.** Every sample record ends in `(Sample)`, for example **Swing Candidates (Sample)**. That marks it as sample data wherever it appears.
- **Frozen and live.** Sample AI reviews and stored reports are labelled `Frozen (Sample)`. Anything you produce yourself is labelled `Live`.
- **Make it operational.** Some chapters end with numbered steps you can follow in the app. Each one has been run against the sample.
- **Screenshots.** Every screenshot shows the sample, captured in the dark theme.
