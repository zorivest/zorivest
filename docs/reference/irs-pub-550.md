# IRS Publication 550 — Investment Income and Expenses

## What Is It?

**IRS Publication 550** is the comprehensive IRS guide covering the tax treatment of investment income and expenses for individual taxpayers. It's the authoritative reference for how capital gains, dividends, interest, and investment expenses are taxed.

---

## Key Topics Covered

### Capital Gains and Losses
- **Short-term**: Held ≤ 1 year → taxed as ordinary income.
- **Long-term**: Held > 1 year → taxed at preferential rates (0%, 15%, or 20%).
- **Net capital loss deduction**: Up to $3,000/year ($1,500 if married filing separately). Excess carries forward.

### Cost Basis
- **Basis** = what you paid for the investment, including commissions and fees.
- **Adjusted basis** = original basis ± adjustments (wash sale disallowed losses, stock splits, dividends reinvested).

### Wash Sales (§4)
- A loss is **disallowed** if you buy the same or substantially identical security within 30 days before or after the sale.
- Disallowed loss is added to the replacement shares' cost basis.
- See wash-sales.md for details.

### Reporting Requirements
- **Form 8949**: Report each sale/exchange.
- **Schedule D**: Summary of capital gains and losses.
- **1099-B**: Broker-provided statement of proceeds.
